# Engine and parser rework plan

One rules compiler from frozen Oracle text to checked Kernel IR. This is the
canonical plan for the rework agreed on 2026-11-27; update it in place as stages
land and move landed contracts into their owning manuals.

**Summary.** Keep the TypeScript grammar that already reads 29,784 of 32,116 Commander-legal identities. Give Rust sole responsibility for deciding what the complete card means and whether the engine can execute it. Start with a measurable evaluator pilot on the unchanged R5 archive, then take the remaining work in the order that finishes unchanged Pool B decks. Judge progress by **whole legal cards supported** and **fully supported Pool B training decks out of 157**. The 2026-11-27 [exclusion rule](#status-and-open-disputes) and its 10 excluded lists are in `generated/rl-train/poolb/poolb-decks.json` under `exclusion`; the Sweep 5 input is preserved byte-for-byte as `poolb-decks.sweep5.json`. Aim for all 157. RL training may start at **150 (95%)** only after those decks pass native admission and deterministic game qualification for the intended training path. Sweep 5 stands at 9,656 legal cards and zero decks. A deck can sit out on a principled rejection and still counts in the denominator. Ambiguous text has a scheduled late stage on the path to 150, because the existing ambiguity flags alone reach 73 training decks. Counts are measured on the frozen Sweep 5 receipts and R5 unless marked projected. Stage thresholds are decision rules.

## What compiles, and who owns the result

**End state (Josh, 2026-11-27).** Every catalog definition comes from text by
one path: TS printed grammar → Rust resolver/checker → checked KIR → evaluator.
Retire all legacy lowerers, compatibility shims, dual readers, bridges and parity
tables. Reach zero audited kernel cells, routing-ladder recognizer pairs,
compiler mirror rejection sites, binder fields and mechanic-named variants of
`Verb`, `DelayedEvent`, `ProposedEvent`, `EventOutcome`, `Reason` and interpreter
stages. Allow only declared, non-decomposable rules-defined primitives as
`BuiltinId` permits, each listed with its CR citation.

Coverage never justifies a shim. When a family cannot migrate cleanly, retire
its old definitions with explicit, located rejections. Accept the coverage loss
and re-earn support through the new path.

One reproducible root command, [`sh scripts/compile-oracle.sh`](game-engine-catalogs.md#frozen-input-compile), verifies input bytes, runs or reuses the verified TS reader, invokes the Rust compiler and publishes the checked catalog, per-identity outcome index and artifact manifest together. Input identity covers exact Oracle text and metadata for all faces, paper and scope evidence, the name catalog, the Comprehensive Rules edition, producer identities and generated schema. An unsupported identity produces a located rejection; an invalid input pin or broken compiler run fails the operation.

- **TypeScript owns the printed language**: ingestion, normalizer, lexer, grammar, face and ability structure, and the versioned [printed-fact schema](oracle_parser.md#ast-archive) that generates the Rust decoder. A mention records what appeared and where; its referent is a Rust decision. Rust re-verifies every span against the original bytes.
- **The syntax archive is a reproducible intermediate.** Rust reads it for compilation; parser tests, corpus diagnostics and fixture tooling may inspect it offline. Product accounts, the deck library, UI and game clients keep their existing interfaces with no semantic dependency on it. A cached archive passes the same byte and schema checks as a fresh one.
- **Rust owns meaning.** Enumerate all in-scope candidates from printed facts before applying eligibility rules. Check domain, cardinality, chooser, timing, visibility, lifetime, zone incarnation and producer/result identity. Resolve a unique justified candidate or return a typed diagnostic with its source location and competing candidates. Bind a result to what actually happened after replacement. Type-check every verb against its signature, then derive execution capabilities. Verify source spans against the original bytes and trace each operative phrase, field and mention to its checked meaning on every required face; one obligation may constrain several instructions. A missing rider, leftover clause, ambiguous candidate or unsupported rule rejects the whole identity. Rust alone certifies a card as fully understood; the archive attests only that the printed text was completely read. The binder's `complete`, `referencesBound` and `fullyBound` flags stop gating as each family migrates.
- **“Supported” has an executable test.** A whole legal card counts once when the frozen identity has a complete checked definition, the build validates it at `Executes`, and the admitted instructions have deterministic implementations and rules witnesses. Record the source, evidence, rules, build, catalog and capabilities behind the result. A deck additionally needs every physical slot and commander, legal designations, tier and capacity checks, native admission, and successful deterministic complete-game qualification under the intended training configuration, exercising the search, observation or analytics capability it actually needs. Report hosting and other tiers independently.

Retain the transaction kernel, casting, priority, combat, state-based actions and trigger placement as the foundation for atomicity, ordering and rollback. Rewrite resolving-ability elaboration, operand evaluation, event records, replacement dispatch and delayed bodies; remove specialized damage and zone handling, TS binder verdicts and projection, the Rust AST mirror, path allowlists, per-verb reference rows and special executors as each checked successor reproduces their choices and proofs. Preserve the old binary, source and regression witnesses.

## Carried forward from the first audits
