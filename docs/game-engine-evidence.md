# Engine evidence and qualification

These source-qualified proofs are separate from [current support](game-engine-current.md),
[mechanics](game-engine-mechanics.md) and [focused checks](game-engine-development.md#choose-focused-checks).
Their counts and gates never transfer to current HEAD or become mandatory checks
for every edit, and none grants later consumer, release, performance or
admission acceptance.

## Evidence authority and reproduction

A claim binds measured source manifest, executable/toolchain/settings, complete
catalog/profile digests, commands/exits, executed selectors, receipt locations
and omissions; a Git SHA alone cannot identify dirty measured bytes, and
compilation is not replay/source verification. Preserve failed/interrupted
attempts and original wrapper status. Corrected readers may authenticate
completed prefixes but cannot relabel failures or transfer acceptance; reuse
requires an unchanged-source bridge. [Storage](game-engine-storage.md) protects
unique ignored/external evidence. Use frozen [catalog inputs](game-engine-catalogs.md),
new output paths and separately qualified native/SDK/browser producers.
