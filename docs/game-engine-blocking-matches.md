## Gameplay and verification boundary

`BlockingCreatureTargetV1` admits the finite blocking-creature spell-pump domain.
The profile does not select different rules. Current target legality, costs,
combat damage, cleanup, privacy, snapshots and replay remain core obligations.
Simulator choices must use public observations and supplied candidates; hidden
blocking membership cannot become a policy input or a new public object flag.

Catalog construction, legal setup, a paid accepted-pod combat witness, simulator
policy behavior and natural full-game completion are distinct verification
obligations. A completed match alone does not establish that Righteousness was
drawn, cast or resolved. A focused accepted-pod witness must retain ordinary
shuffle, opening choices, paid casts and blocker declarations before demonstrating
the boost and replay. A fixed-index completion game and explicit saved-journal
replay exercise the complete development path; any unreached instruction sites
must remain visible.

The Develop policy recognizes positive blocking-target pumps as reactive only
in DeclareBlockers and FirstStrikeDamage priority windows. Before initiation it
uses permitted attacker/blocker links conservatively; these cannot identify an
orphan blocker. Once a target request supplies candidate handles, those candidates
provide membership authority, including for orphans. Current visible type,
targetability and friendly control still guide selection. Ordinary Growth spells
do not become generally reactive. No card/profile identity selects policy rules.
